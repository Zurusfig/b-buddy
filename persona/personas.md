# Budget Buddy - Synthetic Personas

10 personas aged 18-25, 6 months of records (2026-05 to 2026-10). Eval clock: **2026-10-31** ("this month" = 2026-10, "last month" = 2026-09).
Generated by `generate_personas.py` - edit the script, not this file.

## Counting rules (the ground truth follows these)

- **Spending** = `expense` rows. A refund is an `income` row in an *expense* category and is subtracted from that category.
- **Income** = `income` rows in income categories (allowance, salary, part_time, freelance, gift).
- **`transfer` rows are never spending or income**: ATM withdrawals, e-wallet top-ups, credit-card payments, goal deposits (`ออมเงิน: ...`) and goal withdrawals (`ถอนเงินออม: ...`).
- **Soft-deleted rows** (`deletedAt` set) are ignored everywhere.
- All amounts are positive THB; `type` gives the direction.
